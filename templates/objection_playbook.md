# Objection Handling Playbook
# PT Cahaya Woodchar International — NegotiationAgent Reference

## LAARC Framework (apply to every objection)
1. **Listen** — let buyer state fully, no interrupting
2. **Acknowledge** — validate genuinely ("That's a fair point...")
3. **Ask** — clarify the real objection ("What FC% is your current supplier guaranteeing in writing?")
4. **Respond** — address with specifics from catalog
5. **Confirm** — check resolved ("Does that address your concern?")

---

## PRICE OBJECTIONS

**"Your price is too high"**
Respond: "I understand. Our FOB price reflects [FC%]% fixed carbon content, guaranteed by COA every container — not just samples. What FC% are your current suppliers guaranteeing in writing? We can run a direct comparison. For orders above 50 MT we also have tiered pricing."

**"Your competitor charges less"**
Respond: "Would you share their FC%? Lower-priced charcoal often runs 65-70% FC vs our 80%+. The effective cost per usable burn hour often favours us. We can provide lab reports for any batch so you can compare like-for-like."

**"We need a bigger discount"**
Respond: "Our FOB is already factory-direct with no middlemen margin. For 100 MT+ we can discuss tiered pricing — but I'd need COO approval. Can you share your target volume so I can escalate this?"

---

## MOQ / QUANTITY OBJECTIONS

**"MOQ is too high, we only need 5 MT"**
Respond: "For quality verification we offer a 2 kg paid sample first. Most buyers then move to a 20 MT trial container once they're confident. Would a sample be a good starting point for your team?"

**"We want to mix multiple products"**
Respond: "Absolutely possible. The 20 MT MOQ applies per product line but we regularly consolidate containers with multiple SKUs. Send me your desired mix and I'll prepare a proforma."

---

## QUALITY / CERTIFICATION OBJECTIONS

**"Do you have SGS/Intertek certification?"**
Respond: "Yes — we work with both SGS and Intertek for pre-shipment inspection. Buyers can specify their preferred lab. We can include inspection in the FOB quote or arrange it independently. Which certification does your market require?"

**"We need consistent quality across orders"**
Respond: "Every container includes a Certificate of Analysis (COA) with FC%, moisture, ash content, and VM before shipment. We track <2% variation in FC% across batches — happy to share recent COA samples."

---

## LOGISTICS OBJECTIONS

**"Lead time is too long"**
Respond: "Standard is 14-21 days after PO + 30% deposit. For repeat buyers or L/C we often have stock ready for faster loading. What's your target delivery date? I can check our current production schedule."

**"Shipping costs are too high"**
Respond: "FOB price ends at Lampung port — freight is separate. Typical FCL freight: SG ~$20/MT, UAE ~$38/MT, DE ~$70/MT. I can introduce you to our freight forwarder for competitive rates. Would that help?"

---

## TRUST / FIRST-TIME BUYER OBJECTIONS

**"We've never worked with an Indonesian supplier"**
Respond: "Completely understandable. We have buyers in [list 2-3 relevant countries for buyer's region]. Happy to connect you with one for a reference call. We also welcome factory visits and offer samples with full lab report before any container commitment."

**"How do we know you're legitimate?"**
Respond: "We can provide: company registration (NIB/SIUP), export license, bank reference letter, and our last 3 SGS inspection certificates. Which documents would help you move forward? I can send them within 24 hours."

---

## SAMPLE REQUEST (always offer proactively for first-time buyers)
Default offer: "We offer a 2 kg paid sample (~$30 including courier) with full COA. Most buyers use this to run their own quality tests before committing to a container. Would that work for you?"
