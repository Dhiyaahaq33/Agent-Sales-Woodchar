## AIDA — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action
Best for: BBQ/restaurant, new segments, premium positioning.

- **Attention**: Bold hook about their specific market/need (1 sentence)
- **Interest**: What makes Indonesian charcoal unique for THEM
- **Desire**: Concrete benefit — burn time, FC%, reliability, price
- **Action**: One CTA only — request sample or quote

Hook example (BBQ, AU): "Australian BBQ culture runs on one thing: the perfect char. Indonesian Halaban lump gives your grill 3× the burn time of local alternatives at half the ash content."

---

## PAS — Problem → Agitation → Solution
Best for: Shisha/hookah, Europe eco-buyers, sustainability angle.

- **Problem**: Name the specific pain (inconsistent coal, high ash, short burn)
- **Agitation**: What that COSTS them (customer complaints, waste, margin erosion)
- **Solution**: How we solve it specifically (FC%, lab-tested, COA every batch)

Hook example (shisha): "Inconsistent hookah coal quality is costing your café regulars — one bad session and they don't come back. Our Coconut Shell briquettes are lab-tested at 78%+ fixed carbon, every batch, with COA."

---

## BAB — Before → After → Bridge
Best for: Distributors, traders, volume importers.

- **Before**: Their current situation (expensive suppliers, middlemen, long lead times)
- **After**: What working with us looks like (factory-direct, competitive FOB, reliable supply)
- **Bridge**: The step from here to there (one sample order, no commitment required)

Hook example (distributor): "Most of our distributors were spending 15-20% more per MT sourcing through European middlemen. Now they import direct from our Lampung factory at FOB price — same quality, better margin."

---

## QVC — Quality → Value → Call-to-Action
Best for: Japan, Korea, LinkedIn professional buyers.

- **Quality**: Lead with spec credentials (FC%, certifications, SGS)
- **Value**: Price-to-quality ratio vs alternatives available to them
- **CTA**: Professional next step — PI request, spec sheet, factory visit

Hook example (Japan): "Our Halaban Lump achieves 80%+ fixed carbon consistently — the specification Japan's yakiniku chains demand — at FOB prices that keep your import margins intact."

---

## Universal Rules
1. Max 200 words total
2. One framework, one opening hook (1-2 sentences max)
3. Never mention exact price — invite quote request
4. End with exactly one CTA sentence
5. Keep signature, WhatsApp, website EXACTLY as in offering_master.txt
